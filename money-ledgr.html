The idea

Money is easier to manage when you can see all of it

Most people do not have a money problem so much as a visibility problem. The salary is in one place, the EMI in another, the SIP in a third, and a friend still owes you for the trip.

Money Ledgr gives each of those a proper home and then connects them, so one app answers the questions that matter: how much can I spend today, what do I owe, and is my money growing?

Features in detail

Five screens that do the heavy lifting

Investments

See what your investments are really doing

Stop guessing whether you are ahead. Money Ledgr puts your portfolio value, what you put in, and what you have gained or lost on a single screen.

  • Portfolio value, amount invested and profit or loss at a glance
  • Overall return and annual return, with the date it was last updated
  • An allocation chart showing how your money is spread out
  • Holdings, watchlist and dividends counted in one summary

Good for: people who invest regularly and want one place to check progress.

Budgets

Set a limit. Stay inside it.

Give every category a monthly limit and watch your spending against it. The app also turns your remaining balance into a daily allowance, so you always know what is safe to spend today.

  • Total budget and the amount used this month
  • A per-day spending allowance for the days left
  • Limits for categories such as food, transport and rent
  • Progress bars that show how much of each limit is gone

Good for: anyone who reaches the end of the month wondering where the money went.

Plan your funds

Decide where your money goes before you spend it

Start with the money you have available and divide it across expenses, savings and goals. You see what is allocated, what is left and how far along you are.

  • Available, allocated and remaining funds side by side
  • An allocation progress bar that updates as you plan
  • Separate lines for EMI, groceries, savings and an emergency fund
  • Edit or remove any allocation when your plans change

Good for: salaried users who want a plan for each paycheque.

Loans

Know exactly what you owe, and what is owed to you

Keep payables and receivables in one list. Every loan shows the principal, what is still outstanding, the interest and the due date, so nothing slips past you.

  • Total owed with monthly and accrued interest
  • Separate tabs for payables and receivables
  • EMI amount, due date and interest rate for each loan
  • Pay and Schedule buttons right on the loan

Good for: anyone with EMIs, personal loans or money lent to friends.

Split payments

Share costs without the awkward maths

Create a group for a trip, a flat or a night out. Add the expenses, see who owes whom, and mark payments as settled when the money changes hands.

  • An overview of what you owe and what you are owed
  • Groups for trips, events and shared bills
  • Group balance and total spent, with a clear Settled status
  • Invitations so friends can join a group

Good for: trips, shared rent and any group that splits bills.
